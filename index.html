Concise report · October 30

Nearby grain futures remain under harvest pressure. December corn is near $4.49½, roughly 20 cents under the October USDA season-average farm price of $4.70. November soybeans near $12.19 still carry about a 19-cent premium to the $12.00 forecast. Chicago wheat near $6.03¼ sits about 27 cents under the $6.30 anchor. January canola is holding in the mid-to-upper C$780s.

The 2025/26 final marketing-year averages were $4.16 corn and $10.50 soybeans. Against the last decade, the current USDA 2026/27 forecasts still sit above realized averages from the mid-2010s through the early 2020s, though well below the 2021–22 spike. The board has adjusted further to the larger corn supply and is now under the official season averages for corn and wheat.
